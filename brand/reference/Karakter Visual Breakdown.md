- Face remains the primary focal point.

### 7. Additional Notes

# Overall Visual Consistency

## Shared Design Language
- Chibi proportions with oversized heads and compact bodies.
- Large, dark visors with expressive digital faces.
- Rounded astronaut forms and bold outlines.
- Glossy highlights and polished, toy-like surfaces.
- Consistent illustration style across all six characters.

## Color-Coded Personalities
| Character | Dominant Palette | Core Identity |
|---|---|---|
| 01 — Dreamer | Pink, white, yellow | Imagination |
| 02 — Explorer | Blue, white, yellow | Adventure |
| 03 — Grover | Green, mint, white | Nature |
| 04 — Dream Keeper | Purple, lavender, yellow | Sleep |
| 05 — Cheerleader | Orange, cream, coral | Encouragement |
| 06 — Thinker | Blue, cyan, white | Intelligence |

## Rendering Characteristics
- Bright, studio-style illumination.
- Clean silhouettes and saturated accent colors.
- High contrast between dark visors and light-colored suits.
- Clear prop-based visual identities.
- Solid black background isolates the characters and emphasizes their outlines.

## Composition & Presentation
- Each character has one dominant identifying prop or visual feature.
- The six designs maintain consistent proportions and rendering.
- Varied poses and silhouettes provide visual distinction.
- The black background functions as a presentation choice, not necessarily an in-world setting.
